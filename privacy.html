Bolo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Bolo (org.samyarth.bolo) is an Android application built by Samyarth, a NavGurukul initiative, for use in classroom sessions. This policy explains exactly what data Bolo touches and where it goes.

What Bolo does

During a session, Bolo listens via the device microphone, passes the audio to the Android system speech recognition service for transcription, and analyses the resulting text to estimate how much English versus other languages was spoken. The transcript text and per-student English share are stored on the device.

What data Bolo handles

DataWhere it livesWho can see it
Microphone audio Streamed to the Android system speech recognition service. Bolo itself does not store the raw audio. Google Speech Services, per Google's privacy policy.
Transcript text Local database on the device only. Only the user of the device.
Per-student English share and speech time Local database on the device only. Only the user of the device.
Cohort name, student names, session topics Entered by the facilitator. Local database only. Only the user of the device.
Language classification Runs entirely on the device using a bundled ML Kit language-identification model. No text leaves the device for this step. Only the user of the device.
Voice fingerprints Not collected. Bolo does not enrol voices or perform biometric speaker identification. —

What Bolo does not do

  • Bolo does not record or save audio files.
  • Bolo does not send any data to Samyarth, NavGurukul, or any server controlled by the developers.
  • Bolo does not show ads, run analytics, or use third-party trackers.
  • Bolo does not require an account or login.

Third-party processor

Audio is sent to Google Speech Services, part of the Android operating system, for real-time transcription. Google's handling of that audio is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Speech Services data handling

The transcript text Google returns is kept by Bolo on the device. The underlying audio is handled by Google according to their own retention policies and the user's Google account settings.

Network use

Bolo requires an internet connection to transcribe speech. Bolo makes no network requests of its own — only the system speech recogniser uses the network, over a connection encrypted by the operating system.

Permissions Bolo requests

  • Microphone — to capture speech during sessions.
  • Internet, Network state — used by the system speech recogniser.
  • Foreground service, Foreground service microphone, Post notifications — to keep recognition running with the screen off and to show the persistent "Bolo Session Active" notification while a session is live.
  • Vibrate — short haptic feedback on session controls.

How to delete your data

All Bolo data is stored on the device. To delete it:

  1. Open Bolo.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Clear all data & restart setup.
  3. Confirm.

This wipes every cohort, student, session, transcript, and the consent flag from the device. Uninstalling Bolo has the same effect.

Children

Bolo is intended for use by adult facilitators with adult learners (18+). Bolo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
