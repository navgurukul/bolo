# What Bolo does with your voice

A short, honest answer for the people in the room.

## The one-sentence version

**Bolo listens so it can count how many minutes of English the group spoke —
and it forgets the words the moment it's done.**

## What we save

Two things. That's it.

1. **A tiny voice fingerprint** for each student who chooses to enroll. It is
   a string of numbers (256 of them) that helps the app tell *who* is speaking.
   You cannot listen to it. You cannot rebuild a voice from it. It lives only
   on this phone, locked with the phone's own key.
2. **The scoreboard** for each session — minutes of speaking time, minutes of
   English, and the topic. Nothing about *what* you said.

That's the whole list.

## What we never save

- The audio itself. Not even for a second longer than we need to count it.
- A transcript of what you said.
- A list of words you used or stumbled on.
- Anything that names *which* non-English language you switched to.
- Any comparison between you and another student.

## How "never on disk" actually works

The microphone fills a small piece of memory — like a chalkboard, not a notebook.
The app reads the chalkboard, decides "this stretch was English / this wasn't,"
adds to the scoreboard, and wipes the chalkboard. Nothing is ever written to a
file. We will publish the test in Phase 1 that proves this on a real phone.

## When you want out

Open your dashboard and tap **Forget my voice**. Your fingerprint is wiped from
this phone immediately. We cannot recover it because we never had a copy
anywhere else.

You can also ask for everything we know about you — it is a small file of
session times and English percentages, and we will hand it to you.

## What we promise, in plain English

- The mic light stays on whenever Bolo is listening. No surprises.
- Bolo never tells the room who spoke the *least* English. We only say
  *who spoke the most.*
- You can leave at any moment — your fingerprint stays on the phone only as
  long as you want it to.
